🚀 Product Management Case Study | Reimagining YouTube's Privacy Experience

A product thinking project focused on redesigning YouTube's privacy and consent journey to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

This project includes:
• User research and pain point analysis
• Product strategy and UX improvements
• Privacy Hub concept with granular consent management
• Privacy Score to enhance user trust
• Success metrics, rollout strategy, and risk assessment
• High-fidelity UI prototype designed in Figma

Built as a Product Management case study demonstrating user-centric design, analytical thinking, and data-driven decision making.
