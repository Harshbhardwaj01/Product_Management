DPDP Compliance Overview

The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 is India's premium legal framework ensuring user transparency over tracking. This presentation simulator implements three core pillars of the act:

Pillar 1: Granular Opt-Ins

Users must be presented with distinct purpose-specific options. Broad "Accept All" pop-ups are legally compromised and create system mistrust.

Pillar 2: Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdrawing consent must be as simple as granting it. The Dynamic Settings screen acts as an instant toggled data revocation manager.

Pillar 3: Erasure Rights

Under Section 12, users can initiate data erasure. Once requested, YouTube's pipeline must clear historical search caches within specified parameters.
